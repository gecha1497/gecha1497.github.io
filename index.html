About me

Bridging academic knowledge and practical technology.

“My goal is to help learners understand computing concepts, apply them confidently, and build solutions to real problems.”

A teaching and mentoring philosophy focused on clarity, practice, collaboration, and continuous learning.

I hold a BSc and MSc in Computer Science from the University of Gondar. I began my university career as a Graduate Assistant and have served as a lecturer since 2019. I currently lead the Department of Software Engineering in the College of Informatics.

My professional interests include programming, software engineering, web and mobile application development, database systems, computer graphics, and computing education. I also advise student projects, including applied industrial software projects.
